What it does

Your digestion, finally legible.

Six focused tools that turn "I think dairy is bothering me" into evidence you can actually see.

Private by design

Your data stays on your device. No cloud, no tracking, no account required.

Smart correlations

Pearson correlations surface which foods line up with how you feel, automatically.

Food fingerprints

Per-ingredient symptom radars show exactly how each food tends to affect you.

Fast meal logging

A curated, bilingual pantry (EN/DE) of 115+ foods. Tap to log — no typing needed.

Wellness check-ins

A minute-long daily check-in captures discomfort, heartburn, stress, and more.

Works offline

Built local-first with Isar. The whole app runs without a connection.

A tour

Six screens. Zero dashboards to configure.

GutCheck shows up pre-wired with curated foods and sensible defaults so you can log your first meal in under a minute.

Daily dashboard

Per-food symptom profile — know at a glance which symptoms each food correlates with.

Foods that actually line up with your symptoms, ranked by correlation strength and FDR-gated for statistical honesty.

Privacy

Privacy as a default, not a feature.

Health data is sensitive. GutCheck is built so we literally cannot see yours — it never leaves your device in the first place.

Your data never leaves your device

GutCheck stores everything in a local Isar database on your phone. There is no GutCheck server. There is no cloud sync. There is no account to create.

No analytics, no telemetry

The app does not phone home. It does not count screen views, does not log crashes remotely, does not know you exist.

No third-party SDKs

No Google Analytics. No Firebase. No Mixpanel. The only network traffic is what you explicitly trigger (like visiting a link).

You can export and delete anytime

Export your full history to JSON with one tap. Clear sample data or all data from Settings. You are always in control.

Open source, auditable

Every line of code is public under the AGPLv3. You (or anyone) can verify these claims by reading the source.

Questions you might have

Do you have my data?

No. We never receive it. It is stored only on your device.

What happens if I lose my phone?

You'll lose your log (there's no cloud backup by design). We recommend periodically exporting to JSON and keeping a copy somewhere you trust.

Is there a backup/sync feature?

Not yet. If one ships, it will be opt-in, end-to-end encrypted, and work with a provider you choose (e.g. your own storage).

What permissions does the app request?

Only what is strictly needed to function. No location, no contacts, no microphone.

Who runs this?

GutCheck is an open-source project. See the GitHub repository for maintainers and contributors.

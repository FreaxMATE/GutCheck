GutCheck is a private, local-first wellness tracker that helps you understand the connection between what you eat and how you feel.

Log meals from a curated database of ingredients. Track symptoms and wellness on a daily basis. Discover correlations between specific foods and your gut comfort, energy, and overall wellbeing using a built-in statistical engine.

Features:

* Local-first: all your data stays on your device
* No accounts, no cloud sync, no telemetry, no ads
* Curated bilingual ingredient database (English / German)
* Daily wellness tracking (energy, mood, gut comfort)
* Fast meal logging by category with search
* Pearson correlation engine identifies food–symptom links
* Charts to visualize trends over time
* Light / dark theme
* Full German and English UI
* Data export (CSV) for sharing with healthcare providers
* Sample data mode for trying out the app

Open source under AGPL-3.0.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

GutCheck is deliberately small. It logs food, it logs how you feel, and it turns the resulting data into pictures you can act on. That's the whole app.

Logging that gets out of your way

Logging is the part you actually have to do every day — so it needs to be fast. GutCheck ships with a curated, bilingual (EN/DE) pantry of 115+ ingredients, and meal templates so your usual breakfast is one tap.

One-tap ingredients

Tap to toggle — no quantities, no weighing, no friction.

Meal templates

Save a breakfast once, log it in a second for the rest of the year.

1-minute wellness check

Sliders for discomfort, heartburn, stress. Binary for diarrhea.

Insights that connect the dots

Once you have a week of data, GutCheck starts doing the math. Spearman rank correlations with Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction, delayed-effect heatmaps, and per-food radar charts surface the patterns your memory would never spot.

Correlation engine

Spearman rank correlation between each ingredient and each symptom, ranked by strength — FDR-corrected so the top list stays honest.

Food radar

Radar chart per ingredient — shape tells the story at a glance.

Delay heatmap

Immediate vs. 4–12h vs. overnight. Because the body is not always prompt.

Private by construction

GutCheck is local-first. Your meal log never leaves your phone — there's no server that could leak it. The app works fully offline, and exporting your own data is a single tap.

On-device only

Isar database on your phone. No cloud, no analytics, no telemetry.

JSON export

Export your full history anytime — it's your data.

Offline-first

Works on a plane, on the subway, off-grid.

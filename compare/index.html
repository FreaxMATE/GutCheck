How GutCheck stacks up.

We looked at the four apps most people land on when they start tracking food and digestion. Here's an honest side-by-side — including where they're genuinely better than us.

What GutCheck does uniquely

  • • The only option that's local-first AND open source.
  • • Food-fingerprint radar + delay heatmap across 0–4h, 4–12h, 12–24h windows.
  • • No account, no subscription, no telemetry — ever.
  • • Bilingual curated pantry (EN/DE) shipped at launch.
  • • Runs on iOS, Android, Web, Linux, and macOS from one codebase.

Where competitors are better

  • • Cara Care is reimbursable on German statutory insurance and includes CBT / hypnotherapy programs.
  • • MySymptoms has a vastly larger food database and polished PDF reports for doctors.
  • • Bearable covers mental-health and broader symptoms, with real cross-device sync.
  • • Monash FODMAP owns the authoritative low-FODMAP food database.
  • • All four have a long track record and active user communities. We don't, yet.
